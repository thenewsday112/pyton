Yet Serie A had signed its own Polymarket agreement only months earlier, and that partnership was aimed squarely at the United States.</p><p>The same company could be ruled out on a Lazio shirt in Italy while appearing in the league’s American media. Inter and Milan, by comparison, were each receiving around €30 million annually from their respective arrangements.</p><p>By July, the optimism had faded. Italy’s Customs and Monopolies Agency, known as ADM, had again blocked access to the platform, treating it as an unauthorised gambling operator. Whether a prediction market belongs in that category has been central to the Polymarket dispute. Readers can see how these distinctions work in practice <a href="https://www.cbssports.com/prediction/news/polymarket-promo-code/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">at CBS Sports Polymarket review</a>, which outlines US eligibility requirements, location restrictions and promotional terms. The league’s deal was expressly regional, aimed at American supporters rather than Italian customers.</p><p>The agreements moved in different directions over the summer:</p><ul> <li>14 May: Serie A USA announced its American partnership with Polymarket.</li> <li>July: Italian restrictions again put Lazio’s shirt deal in doubt.</li> <li>11 August: Lazio and Polymarket agreed to end their sponsorship.</li> </ul><p>Lazio’s announcement concerned the club’s contract. Reports valued that season’s portion at approximately €9.5 million.</p><p>That protects one season’s fee, but the shirt space still needs a new buyer for future campaigns. The club and Polymarket also left open the possibility of working together again if the regulatory position changes.</p><h1>A Contract Must Work Where the Football Is Played</h1><p>Serie A clubs are looking beyond Italy for new commercial income, particularly in the American market. Regional media partnerships allow the league to reach that audience without necessarily placing the same branding in Italian stadiums.</p><p>Lazio’s experience shows why those distinctions need to be written into agreements from the outset.
