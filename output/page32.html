Lil Baby Is Open to ‘Stepping Out of the Box’ to Collab With Sabrina Carpenter: ‘I Could Make It Work’

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

What used to be a staple of pop music — the rapper/pop star collaboration — is basically dead. However, Lil Baby is willing to do his part to have the sector be revitalized.

Baby sat down with The New York Times’ Popcast on Thursday for an extensive conversation, which spilled into his aspirations to try stepping outside of his comfort zone for a glossy collaboration with a pop star like Sabrina Carpenter.

“I’m still in that box a little bit, but I’m more open to stepping out of the box,” he said. “The box is bigger. As I step out a little bit, the box gets bigger.

Baby continued: “Even a Sabrina Carpenter — just say, for instance, in my head, I don’t have a song on my hard drive to send to her. But if we sit there and create something, or she sends me something, I feel like I could make it work.”

Pop star/rapper collaborations have become a rarity. Camila Cabello has been attempting to keep the crossover team-ups alive. The former Fifth Harmony singer recruited Drake for “Hot Uptown” and “Uuugly,” while also joining forces with Playboi Carti for “I Luv It” (No. 81 Billboard Hot 100 peak). All three were released in 2024 and landed on her C,XOXO album, which debuted at No. 13 on the Billboard 200.

Dua Lipa enlisted DaBaby for the dynamic “Levitating” remix in 2020, which dominated commercially, reaching No. 2 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 2021.

Sabrina Carpenter does actually have a rap collab under her belt, clashing with Saweetie for “I Can’t Stop Me,” which landed on SC’s Singular Act II project in 2019.

As for Lil Baby, he doesn’t have Sabrina or any pop stars on his upcoming Fresh album, which is set to arrive on Nov. 6. Fans of the 4PF rapper can expect guest appearances from Future, Young Thug and Rylo Rodriguez.

North West Reveals How She Makes Beats & Her Favorite Song on Her Dad’s ‘Graduation’ Album

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

North West recently made an appearance on one of her favorite YouTube shows and revealed her favorite song off her father’s classic album Graduation, while also revealing a bunch of different talents.

It’s no secret that Ye (formerly Kanye West) and Kim Kardashian‘s daughter is talented and has been making music on her own. While sitting down with Well Well Well host Emwell, she talked about her production process a bit. “Usually, I listen to a lot of music first, and I feel like what type of song I want to do, like what type of beat first,” she said before mentioning the software program she prefers using. “And then I could do like a sample. Usually, doing a sample is so much easier than starting from scratch. I use Ableton, but I used to play on an ASR. I only use Ableton right now.”

Later in the interview, Emwell showed her a Billboard Hip-Hop Instagram post highlighting an article about Ye crediting her for helping him find the love for music again. She shared that she thought the comment was heartfelt, but then remembered that she was in the middle of rap camp when he said it. “When I saw it, I was like, ‘Aw, that’s nice,'” North said. “Well, actually, when he said that, I was going to the studio every day with him. It was like low-key training in Japan. Ugh, oh my gosh, I did the craziest really fast rapping. He was like, ‘Oh, you gotta learn how to do it a cappella,’ and it was just so hard. Oh my gosh, but that’s what got me better, so I’m high-key grateful.”

Emwell then showed her a picture of her Halloween costume back in 2023 when she dressed up like the Graduation bear and asked her favorite song on the album to which she replied, “‘Homecoming,'” and then started to sing the chorus.

And as for her other talents? She can draw on the spot, has been learning how to make wigs and can speak with her mouth closed.
