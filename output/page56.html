Alan Shearer makes Liverpool Premier League title claim ahead of Arsenal clash

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →
Former Newcastle United striker Alan Shearer (Picture: PA)

Alan Shearer thinks Liverpool can be considered genuine Premier League title challengers if they are to beat Arsenal on Sunday.

Andoni Iraola’s side have bounced back impressively from their 3-0 defeat to Manchester City earlier this month, winning five straight games in all competitions.

The Reds impressively beat third-place Brighton last weekend and will look to further close the gap on the top two when they host defending champions Arsenal at Anfield.

Dominik Szoboszlai’s thunderous free-kick helped Liverpool claim a narrow 1-0 win when the two sides met on Merseyside last season.

And former Newcastle United striker Shearer believes that Liverpool can throw their name into the title mix if they extend their impressive home record against the Gunners this weekend.

‘They’re just little signs of Liverpool getting into stride,’ Shearer told Betfair.

‘I worked their game against Villarreal and for half an hour, they were really poor but after that there was a big improvement. They got a brilliant result against Brighton. Not many people thought that they could get that result.

Liverpool have won five matches in a row (Picture: Shutterstock)

‘It just feels as if there’s a little bit of momentum happening at Liverpool. It seems as if the players are buying into his ideas and they’re getting results.

‘Whether you like it or not Liverpool could become title challengers with a win on Sunday, you have to look at it that way.

‘Now, whatever happens with Man City, whenever it happens, I guess the team in third position would also have to think, well, if that does happen to City, then we’ve got to be ready. We’ve got to be ready to mount a challenge, you know?

‘So, I think it’s a big opportunity for Liverpool to throw themselves into that talk if they get the right result this weekend.’

Arsenal are looking to bounce back from a shock midweek defeat in the Carabao Cup (Picture: Shutterstock)

Arsenal's dismal Anfield record

Arsenal are winless in their last 13 away Premier League games against Liverpool (D5 L8) since winning 2-0 in 2012-13.

They’ve never gone 14 winless away at Anfield in their league history, also going 13 between 1975-76 and 1987-88, a run which was broken by their famous 2-0 final day victory in 1988-89 which secured the title.

(Opta)

Arsenal, meanwhile, come into this fixture off the back of a shock Carabao Cup defeat to League Two side Fleetwood Town, and Shearer is not confident of a positive outcome for Mikel Arteta’s side.

‘Arsenal need to bounce back on Sunday,’ he added.

‘They got a good result last weekend which was pretty straightforward against Everton but I guess the criticism after Tuesday is warranted.

‘I know Arteta put a different team out, but still you’re representing Arsenal and it was an embarrassing result.

‘Arsenal now have got the chance to bounce back, but I guess the game you wouldn’t want would be going to Anfield as Liverpool just feel as if they’ve got a bit of form going, a bit of momentum going.

‘It doesn’t come much tougher than going to Anfield for Arsenal. I don’t think they’ve got the best record there either. I don’t think they’ve won there in a while.’

Barcelona targeting January move for Tottenham Hotspur defender

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Tottenham could face growing interest in one of their key players ahead of the January transfer window, with Barcelona president Joan Laporta reportedly keen on bringing Pedro Porro to the Nou Camp.

The Spanish right-back has attracted attention following his performances for Spain at the World Cup, but Spurs have several reasons to feel confident about keeping hold of the defender.

Barcelona Identify Pedro Porro as January Target

According to Catalan broadcaster 3Cat, Porro is one of Laporta’s preferred defensive targets as Barcelona consider whether to strengthen their backline in January.

The Catalan club have been dealing with a series of defensive injuries involving Eric Garcia, Jules Kounde, Andreas Christensen and Xavi Espart. However, Barcelona have not made a final decision on whether to bring in another defender, with the club waiting to assess the injured players’ recoveries.

Laporta’s admiration for Porro reportedly grew during Spain’s World Cup campaign. The Tottenham defender became an important part of the national team, developing a strong understanding with Lamine Yamal on the right flank and scoring twice during the tournament, including in the semi-final against France.

Laporta watched that semi-final in Dallas and was reportedly impressed by Porro’s performance. Barcelona had also gathered information about the defender towards the end of the previous summer transfer window, but their enquiry did not progress into a formal proposal to either the player or Tottenham.

Porro’s contractual situation gives Spurs a strong negotiating position. The 27-year-old renewed his contract shortly before the World Cup, committing his future to the north London club until 2031.

Reports have also indicated that Tottenham would demand a significant fee to consider allowing the defender to leave. Real Madrid have also been linked with an interest in the Spain international, potentially creating further competition for his signature.

What This Means for Tottenham

Barcelona’s admiration for Porro is a reminder of the defender’s standing in European football, but there is currently no confirmed bid or formal approach.

Tottenham will be keen to retain a player who remains an important part of their plans. His long-term contract also means the club are under no immediate pressure to negotiate a sale.

The situation could develop if Barcelona decide their defensive injury problems require January reinforcements. For now, however, Spurs remain in a strong position, and any interested club would likely have to make a substantial offer to test their resolve.

Manchester City favourites to sign Borussia Dortmund star after formal approach

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Manchester City are continuing to invest in young talent as they look to strengthen their squad for the future, with another exciting prospect emerging on their transfer radar.

Pep Guardiola’s side have reportedly moved into pole position for one of Borussia Dortmund’s most highly rated youngsters.

United States international Mathis Albert has attracted interest from several European clubs, but City are currently considered the favourites to secure his signature.

With the teenager’s first-team opportunities limited in Germany, the Premier League champions could look to take advantage of the situation.

Manchester City Lead Mathis Albert Chase

According to TEAMtalk, Manchester City are the frontrunners to sign Albert after making a formal approach during the summer transfer window.

The report claims City are prepared to offer the 17-year-old lucrative personal terms as they step up their pursuit. Their interest is serious, with the club previously prepared to meet a valuation in the region of €20 million (£16.7m).

Chelsea are also long-term admirers of the youngster and were reportedly willing to spend a similar amount last winter. Paris Saint-Germain previously made contact with Dortmund in 2025, while Arsenal and Liverpool have more recently been monitoring his situation.

However, City are currently viewed as being in the strongest position should Dortmund soften their stance.

Albert joined Borussia Dortmund from the LA Galaxy academy in 2024 and has quickly established himself as one of the most exciting young American talents in European football. He has already made Bundesliga appearances and earned senior international recognition with the United States.

Despite his potential, opportunities under Niko Kovac have been limited this season. That lack of regular first-team football has encouraged several clubs to monitor his development, although Dortmund remain determined to keep hold of their young attacker.

The Bundesliga side have reportedly rejected approaches, including loan enquiries, and continue to view Albert as an important part of their long-term plans. They are also preparing a contract extension offer, with his current agreement running until June 2027.

There is another important factor for City. Premier League regulations prevent English clubs from signing Albert permanently until he turns 18 in May, meaning any potential transfer would be geared towards next summer rather than an immediate move.

Manchester United targeting Dominic Calvert-Lewin in surprise January transfer move

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Manchester United could be forced to strengthen their attacking options in January after another injury setback involving Benjamin Sesko.

With Michael Carrick needing additional firepower to maintain competition for places up front, an experienced Premier League striker has reportedly emerged as a surprise target.

Leeds United forward Dominic Calvert-Lewin is among the names being considered by United’s recruitment team. However, attempting to sign a key player from one of their biggest rivals could prove extremely difficult.

Manchester United Consider Dominic Calvert-Lewin

According to The Athletic, Manchester United are exploring the January market for centre-forwards, with Calvert-Lewin one of the players under consideration.

The 29-year-old England international has reportedly been on United’s radar for some time, and recruitment staff are now assessing whether a potential transfer would be feasible when the window opens.

The interest comes amid concerns over Benjamin Sesko’s availability. The Slovenian striker is expected to miss an extended period with a shin injury, leaving Carrick with fewer recognised centre-forward options.

Calvert-Lewin has rebuilt his career since joining Leeds on a free transfer from Everton in 2025. His performances at Elland Road have attracted renewed attention, with the striker scoring 20 goals in 49 appearances for the club across all competitions.

He has also returned to England international contention following his improved form, making him an appealing option for United as they consider their attacking requirements.

However, there are significant obstacles. Leeds are reportedly keen to reward Calvert-Lewin with a new contract, despite his current agreement running until 2028. The club are also understood to be protective of a player who has become an important part of Daniel Farke’s side.

The rivalry between Leeds and Manchester United presents another major complication. A transfer between the clubs would attract considerable attention, and reports suggest the possibility of an agreement is currently remote.

What This Means for Manchester United

Calvert-Lewin could offer United a different profile in attack, bringing physical presence, aerial ability and considerable Premier League experience. His availability could become particularly important if Sesko remains sidelined into the January window.

United have previously considered alternative centre-forward options, but the latest developments could encourage the recruitment department to widen its search. Calvert-Lewin’s experience in English football would potentially make him an attractive short-term solution.

Nevertheless, Leeds are unlikely to make negotiations straightforward. They have little reason to weaken their squad midway through the campaign, particularly by allowing an important player to join their historic rivals.

United must also consider whether pursuing Calvert-Lewin is realistic when other striker targets may be easier to acquire.

For now, the reported interest represents an option under consideration rather than an imminent transfer. Sesko’s injury has increased the urgency surrounding United’s search for attacking reinforcements, but persuading Leeds to sell their striker could prove a significant challenge.

Chelsea targeting Trent Alexander-Arnold as surprise transfer opportunity emerges

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Chelsea could be preparing for a significant change at right-back as the club assess their options ahead of the upcoming transfer windows.

With uncertainty surrounding Malo Gusto’s future at Stamford Bridge, the Blues have reportedly identified an unexpected target who could bring considerable experience and creativity to their defence.

Real Madrid defender Trent Alexander-Arnold has emerged as a potential option, with former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown suggesting the England international could be tempted by a move to west London if Chelsea decide to make an approach.

Chelsea Monitoring Trent Alexander-Arnold

According to Football Insider, Chelsea are keeping tabs on Alexander-Arnold as questions continue over his role at Real Madrid. The 28-year-old has struggled to establish himself as a regular starter under Jose Mourinho, with competition for places limiting his opportunities.

Brown revealed that Chelsea are among the clubs monitoring the situation, telling Football Insider: “One name who I have heard in connection with a move to Chelsea is Trent Alexander-Arnold.”

He admitted the potential transfer had initially surprised him, adding: “It came as a bit of a surprise to me, to be honest, but he has fallen well down the pecking order at Real Madrid to the point where he’s now the second or even third choice.”

The former scout also questioned whether the defender currently fits into Mourinho’s plans, explaining:

“It appears Mourinho doesn’t rate him particularly in that right-back position, and he’s not the only one, because he’s been out in the cold for England as well.”

Despite those concerns, Brown believes Alexander-Arnold remains an attractive player because of his technical ability and reputation. Chelsea, meanwhile, have reportedly been assessing potential replacements for Gusto as they consider changes to their defensive options.

Brown added: “Alexander-Arnold is a talented footballer who has a very good skillset, but it is those weaknesses in his game that often raises concerns.”

He also suggested that Chelsea’s interest in high-profile players could make the move possible: “Chelsea, though, will always be keen on the possibility of signing a big-name player; it’s just what they do, so they’ll be keeping an eye on whether he does become available.”

The possibility of a return to Liverpool has also been discussed, but Brown believes Stamford Bridge could become a realistic destination if the defender decides to leave Spain.

“They’ll want to avoid that situation coming up again, as will he I expect, so Chelsea could be where he ends up and he could be tempted if they decide to make a move.”

However, Alexander-Arnold is not currently pushing for an exit. Previous reporting has suggested he wants to fight for his place at Real Madrid, meaning any potential Chelsea move would depend on how his situation develops.
