Sky: From Galliani to Kaka – Cardinale treated Milan figures to dinner to explain project

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

By: Isak Möller

Gerry Cardinale hosted a dinner on the eve of the big derby clash between AC Milan and Inter, explaining his projects to Adriano Galliani, Ricky Kaka, Ezio Simonelli and many others.

Cardinale has been in Italy for the last week or so, attending the new stadium unveiling as well as the games against Udinese and Bologna. Tonight, he will be at San Siro for the derby and to mark the big occasion, he hosted a big dinner last night.

According to Peppe Di Stefano of Sky, as relayed by MilanVibes, Cardinale invited about 60 people to dinner. It wasn’t just an opportunity to catch up, but also for the American to explain what he has planned for Milan, seeking the support of some club legends.

High-profile dinner

As mentioned at the top, the likes of Galliani, Kaka and the Serie A president Simonelli attended the dinner. Other notable Milan legends present were Marcel Desailly, Serginho, Cristian Brocchi, Mathieu Flamini, Mauro Tassotti, Filippo Galli and Daniele Massaro.

It was definitely nice to see Galliani turn up at the dinner, especially since he bumped into Kaka outside and we thus got a chance to see their warm embrace. The two still have a great relationship after experiencing so much success together over the years.

Udinese vs. Roma probable line-ups and where to watch

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Donyell Malen leads the line for Roma as Gian Piero Gasperini’s side visit Udinese, with Paulo Dybala still out. Nicolò Zaniolo faces his former club for the Bianconeri.

It kicks off at 18:00 CET [17:00 GMT] at the Bluenergy Stadium, with Fabio Maresca refereeing.

Roma are third on 21 points and still unbeaten in Serie A. They beat Cagliari 4-2 at the Olimpico on Tuesday, with Lulli, Niccolò Pisilli, Rodrigo Mora and Malen all scoring. Malen has seven league goals this season from just 12 shots on target. The Giallorossi travel to Manchester United in the Champions League on Tuesday.

Udinese ended a run of six straight league defeats with Wednesday’s 0-0 draw at Parma. Maduka Okoye saved a penalty from Adrián Bernabé in that game. Kosta Runjaic’s side are on five points, and his position is still under scrutiny with a trip to Venezia to come after this.

Udinese vs. Roma – team news

“Roma are fighting for the Scudetto, they’re a strong side, but against the big teams you never start without hope,” Runjaic said. He added that Zaniolo and Keinan Davis would both play despite not being fully fit.

Dybala (calf) is not in Roma’s squad. Mile Svilar (toe), Manu Koné (hamstring) and Lorenzo Pellegrini (hamstring) are also out. Gasperini has called up two Primavera players, Di Nunzio and Camara.

Udinese are without Oumar Solet, Bayo, David Alaba, Gueye and Alessandro Zanoli. Solet and Bayo have returned to training and are aiming for the Venezia game. Bertola is available despite finishing the Parma match exhausted.

Neither side has any players suspended. Jesper Karlström is one booking away from a ban.

ROME, ITALY – OCTOBER 27: Emanuele Lulli of AS Roma celebrates scoring his team’s first goal with teammate Gianluca Mancini during the Serie A match between AS Roma and Cagliari Calcio at Stadio Olimpico on October 27, 2026 in Rome, Italy. (Photo by Paolo Bruno/Getty Images)

There are plenty of selection doubts in the Roma defence:

According to Tuttosport, Gianluca Mancini starts alongside Evan Ndicka and Mario Hermoso. Voce Giallorossa has Ghilardi in for Mancini. Meanwhile, Fantacalcio expects Leonardo Balerdi to replace Ndicka after recovering from the knock he took at Napoli.

Wesley is set to return on the left. Bryan Cristante is expected to start in midfield alongside either Pisilli or Marten de Roon, and Mora and Matías Soulé support Malen.

For Udinese, Runjaic has said there will be only minor changes from the Parma draw. Jakub Piotrowski is battling Chakvetadze for a midfield spot, as Piotrowski isn’t yet fit to last 90 minutes.

Udinese vs. Roma – where to watch on TV

The match is live on DAZN in the UK and Ireland, and on Paramount+ in the USA. Football Italia will provide live updates via a live blog, with Udinese-Roma followed by Milan-Inter.

When Michael Carrick made major decision on Man United’s Bruno Fernandes

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Manchester United manager Michael Carrick reportedly decided to drop Bruno Fernandes before his public outburst at the end of the Como game.

The Portugal international has been a star player for Man Utd for many years, but he landed himself in trouble by criticising the tactics of Carrick and his coaching staff in that 4-1 loss away to Como in the Champions League.

Carrick subbed Fernandes off in the 67th minute of that game, and he was then named on the bench for the defeat against Bournemouth in the Premier League that weekend.

Still, according to the Daily Mail, it seems that Carrick actually came to the decision to drop Fernandes before hearing about his post-match comments, as he seemed prepared to axe him based on his performance for just over an hour against Como.

Is it time for Manchester United to move on from Bruno Fernandes?

United fans will likely have mixed feelings about how the Fernandes and Carrick saga has played out, but it might make more sense than the manager has been given credit for.

As much as MUFC fans might not like to admit it, they probably need to take a serious look at how Arsenal have rebuilt themselves under Mikel Arteta in recent times, and that involved making some difficult and initially unpopular decisions.

(Photo by Carl Recine/Getty Images)

The Gunners boss quite quickly ostracized Mesut Ozil from his squad, and later Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang, and there might be some similarities with Fernandes at Old Trafford.

Even if the 32-year-old is a world class player on his day, if he’s going to throw a tantrum after bad results and shift blame away from himself and onto the manager, then this United project is never going to go anywhere.
