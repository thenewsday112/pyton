Ruben Amorim made it clear yesterday that the Scudetto remains the goal, but if the gap is increased to ten, it will be tough.</p><p>It’s true that Stefano Pioli, in February 2022, also risked going ten points behind until Olivier Giroud turned the game around. For Milan, the midfielders are crucial with their goals.</p><p>The midfield is indeed the centre of Milan’s power, where the Ballon d’Or winner Luka Modric reigns supreme and where counterattacks that end up in goal are born. Amorim’s choices in this department weren’t easy.</p><p>The most <a href="https://sempremilan.com/serie-a-preview-milan-inter-2026-27" rel=""><strong>tested on the eve of the match</strong></a>: a 4-3-3 with Musah and Rabiot on either side of Modric, and an attacking trident of Moreira-Ramos-Pulisic.</p><figure class="wp-caption alignnone"><source type="image/webp" /><img alt="Adrien Rabiot of Milan" class="wp-image-345498 has-webp" height="750" src="https://icdn.sempremilan.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/SalisburgoMilan-19.jpg" width="1200" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text">Photo by AC Milan</figcaption></figure><p>There were fewer doubts for Chivu who has a tested midfield and, in general, the most coherent team in the league. But that doesn’t mean they don’t have problems. Against Venezia, Inter suffered six dangerous transitions, against Roma there were seven.</p><p>In the middle of the pitch, due to the pressure of pressing or laziness in the return, there have often been gaps to exploit. The centre-backs have dipped in form and the conceded xG is much higher, which Milan will try to exploit.</p><p>Ramos is pushing hard to score his third league goal, which he desperately needs despite adding a lot to Milan’s game: 14 balls recovered (Thuram 4, Lautaro 5, Pio 8), 21 lay-offs (Thuram 7, Lautaro 8, Pio 13). All he has to do is find the net.</p><figure class="wp-caption alignnone"><source type="image/webp" /><img alt="Christian Pulisic of AC Milan" class="wp-image-346629 has-webp" height="751" src="https://icdn.sempremilan.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ac-milan-v-bologna-fc-serie-a-2026-27-4.jpg" width="1200" /><figcaption class="wp-caption-text">Photo by Marco Luzzani/Getty Images</figcaption></figure><p>However, if the player that Milan fear the most is Thuram, then that player is Pulisic for Inter.
