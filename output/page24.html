Sheffield Wednesday are considering a move for Jimmy Thelin in their hunt for a new manager, according to a report by The Daily Record.

Sheffield Wednesday host Blackpool at Hillsborough looking to build on their recent home form and move closer to the League One play-off places. The Owls currently sit ninth with 19 points from 12 matches, having recorded five wins and four draws.

Matt Hamshaw remains in interim charge following the dismissal of Henrik Pedersen, with SWFC still on the hunt for a new boss. A new name has appeared on the radar of Wednesday fans though, with a report from The Daily Record claiming they are considering a move for Jimmy Thelin.

Photo by ClawIsland95, via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

They have said that Thelin is among the leading candidates for the job and that the Owls are keen to hold talks with him. Those reports have also been backed up by Swedish outlet Expressen.

The 48-year-old boss has been without a job since he left Aberdeen back in January. He has since spent time away from football and turned down jobs in Scandinavia and Europe with an eye on a move to England.

Why might Sheffield Wednesday be so keen on Jimmy Thelin?

Thelin has some imrpessive feats during his managerial career so far. He began his managerial career in Sweden in 2005 before joining Jonkopings Sodra IF’s youth setup four years later.

The 48-year-old was promoted to first-team manager in 2014 and guided the club to promotion to the Allsvenskan in 2015, ending a 46-year absence from the Swedish top flight.

He subsequently spent over six years at IF Elfsborg, establishing himself as one of Sweden’s promising coaches. His biggest achievement came in 2023 when he led the club to an Allsvenskan runners-up finish, narrowly missing out on the title on the final day. He was also named the division’s Manager of the Year.

Thelin moved to Aberdeen in 2024, enjoying an impressive start before winning the Scottish Cup in 2025 by beating Celtic on penalties. After the cup win, he then took Aberdeen into the UEFA conference League however, declining results eventually led to his dismissal in January 2026.

The Verdict: Jimmy Thelin may be the most exciting Sheffield Wednesday candidate yet

There have not been too many concrete candidates named for the Sheffield Wednesday job yet. Dave Challinor was the obvious name when Pedersen was sacked, given his history with sporting director Simon Wilson.

After Challinor though there has not been much further talk. There is admiration for Notts County boss Martin Paterson and Hull City assistant Dean Holden was also of interest but he is set to sign a new deal with the Premier League outfit.

That leaves Thelin as they only other reported candidate. His previous stints with Aberdeen and in Scandinavia leave him as a somewhat standout along with Challinor.

Google's First Data Center Satellite Is Online and Writing Poetry

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →
Google's first experimental space-based data center is operational after launching earlier this month, successfully running its Gemma 4 AI model aboard a satellite and generating a haiku about being in orbit. TechCrunch reports: The satellite, which carries four Google Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) and generates up to 1 kW of electricity, is intended to pave the way for future networks of close-flying satellites performing large inference workloads in orbit. However, the power demands of the TPU mean it can only turn one on for about 20 minutes at a time, complicating the already tricky business of space-to-ground communications. After a commissioning process that tested out the satellite's subsystems over about three weeks, the spacecraft was ready to work. When engineers on the ground sent their first prompt this week, they asked the model pre-loaded onto the satellite, Google DeepMind's Gemma 4, to write a haiku about being in space. And thus: Blue marble below Data streams in silent grace Starlight feeds my core. The ongoing test campaign will examine how the chips perform under thermal stress and in the space radiation environment. [...] If the TPUs onboard this spacecraft prove capable, the next step will be launching two more satellites equipped with the advanced chips in 2027. Those spacecraft will support higher power generation and demonstrate inter-satellite optical links.

Social Security's Trump Bump-Led 2027 COLA Is Doing Something That Hasn't Been Witnessed This Century

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

For a majority of retirees, Social Security represents more than just a monthly check. The income Social Security's roughly 55 million retired-worker beneficiaries receive monthly is often vital to making ends meet.

Given how important Social Security income is to the financial well-being of our nation's aging workforce, few announcements are more anticipated than the annual cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) reveal in October. Social Security's COLA is essentially the near-annual "raise" passed on to recipients that accounts for the inflation (rising prices) they've faced. If benefits didn't adjust for inflation, retirees' buying power would decline over time.
