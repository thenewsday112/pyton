Harry Styles Revives His ‘X Factor’ Audition Look for ‘Isn’t She Lovely’ at Harryween

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Harry Styles went back to where it all started for Harryween.

At Madison Square Garden on Friday (Oct. 30), the 29th night of his 30-show Together, Together residency, Styles changed into the outfit he wore for his 2010 X Factor audition and performed Stevie Wonder’s “Isn’t She Lovely,” the song that got him through to the next round at 16. He even repeated a line from that day, telling the crowd, “I work in a bakery,” a nod to the part-time job he held in his Cheshire hometown of Holmes Chapel.

The audition outfit was a mid-show swap. Styles opened the night dressed as Elvis Presley, while his band and dancers came as different versions of Styles from across his career. The Elvis nod tracks: the first song Styles ever recorded was a cover of Presley’s “The Girl of My Best Friend.”

On the audition itself, “Isn’t She Lovely” wasn’t Styles’ first pick. Footage released by the show in 2022 revealed he opened with Train’s “Hey, Soul Sister,” and only sang the Wonder song a cappella after Simon Cowell asked to hear him without a backing track. He was later placed in One Direction alongside Niall Horan, Louis Tomlinson, Zayn Malik and the late Liam Payne. Earlier this month, Styles debuted “What Is Enough,” a song written for Payne, at the same venue on the second anniversary of Payne’s death.

Costumed covers have become a Harryween tradition. At the first edition, at MSG in 2021, Styles dressed as Dorothy to sing “Over the Rainbow,” then came out as a clown the next night to cover Britney Spears’ “Toxic.” In 2022, at Los Angeles’ Kia Forum, he dressed as Grease’s Danny Zuko for “Hopelessly Devoted to You.”

The residency wraps Saturday (Oct. 31) with a final Harryween show. The MSG run drew a record 11.5 million presale registrations on Ticketmaster. Brooklyn Beckham has no time for haters — especially if criticism is directed at his wife, Nicola Peltz Beckham.

Sharing a candid photo of Nicola, 31, via his Instagram Story on Friday, October 30, Brooklyn, 27, took aim at trolls before they had a chance to make any snarky comments.

“Yea I am obsessed with my wife,” he wrote via the post. “Bring on the comments, I don’t care, she’s perfect.”

The Cloud23 hot sauce founder ended the message with a middle finger emoji and red heart emoji to further emphasize his intended sentiment.

Brooklyn Beckham Calls Wife His Everything After Blocking Parents on Instagram

Brooklyn has never shied away from defending his wife, who he married in Palm Beach, Florida in April 2022. When an Instagram commenter body-shamed Nicola in August, Brooklyn was having none of it.

“Do you comment on everyone who is concerned about your wife and how sickly she looks? She puts herself out there; feed her,” a social media user commented underneath an unrelated post about Brooklyn’s cottage pie recipe.

In response, Brooklyn hit back, “Don’t ever talk about my wife. She’s absolutely beautiful and you should never comment on a woman’s body. It’s disgraceful.”

Brooklyn Beckham and Nicola Peltz Beckham. (Photo by Aurore Marechal/Getty Images)

The couple have been putting on a united front in all areas of their life, particularly amid Brooklyn’s estrangement from his family, including parents David and Victoria Beckham. (As well as Brooklyn, David and Victoria are parents to Romeo, 24, Cruz, 21, and Harper, 15.)

Brooklyn slammed his family in a scathing social media post uploaded via his Instagram Stories on January 19.

“I have been silent for years and made every effort to keep these matters private,” Brooklyn wrote. “Unfortunately, my parents and their team have continued to go to the press, leaving me with no choice but to speak for myself and tell the truth about only some of the lies that have been printed.”

Nicola Peltz Felt 'So Lucky' to Spend Time With Beckham Family Before Rift

“I do not want to reconcile with my family. I’m not being controlled, I’m standing up for myself for the first time in my life,” he wrote.

Brooklyn also claimed that David and Victoria have “controlled narratives in the press” about their family for his “entire life.”

Additionally, he alleged that his parents have tried to “ruin [his] relationship” with Nicola prior to their wedding. During their nuptials, Brooklyn claimed Posh Spice allegedly ”hijacked” her son and daughter-in-law’s first dance and “danced inappropriately on [Brooklyn] in front of everyone.”

As for David, Brooklyn claimed that his soccer star dad “rejected” him and Nicola when they visited him for his 50th birthday in 2025, causing more tension.
