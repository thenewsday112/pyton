Fujifilm to boost tape production 5x as AI fuels archiving demand

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

In an announcement released Thursday, Fujifilm said it will invest roughly 10 billion yen (about $63 million) to increase its production capacity fivefold for an unlikely beneficiary of the AI era: magnetic tape.

Fujifilm attributes the surge in archiving demand to the spread of generative AI, which the company remarked is producing "exponential growth" in data needs worldwide. The 5x increase in production capacity will be measured against fiscal year 2025 production levels.

The investment in Fujifilm's Kanagawa Factory, Odawara site will make it "the largest investment Fujifilm has made to date in dedicated LTO tape facilities." The project will run from fiscal years 2026 through 2028 to fund manufacturing expansion and additional personnel, primarily to produce the latest-generation "FUJIFILM LTO Ultrium10" tape.

Four Man Utd stars set to miss Chelsea clash with travelling squad revealed

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →
Mainoo was with the United squad boarding the train for London (Picture: Eamonn & James Clarke)

Kobbie Mainoo is poised to return from injury for Manchester United against Chelsea on Saturday but Michael Carrick is set to remain without four other stars.

The United midfielder has missed his side’s last four games – with his side losing four and drawing the other during that run – due to a muscle injury.

The 21-year-old was back in full training on Wednesday morning, leaving him in the frame to return to action at Stamford Bridge on Saturday afternoon.

‘[In terms of] good news, Kobbie has done a little bit of training yesterday. So, he’s back and around the group, so that’s obviously positive,’ Carrick told a press conference on Thursday.

Mainoo was among the 22-man United squad pictured boarding the train to the capital from Stockport Station on Friday.

Marcus Rashford and Luke Shaw were not spotted among the group, however. Details over Shaw’s latest issue have been scarce, also missing his side’s last four games having last featured in the 1-1 draw with Tottenham.

His absence has caused havoc at left-back with the club’s decision not to sign another player for that position in the summer once again thrown under the spotlight.

Rashford meanwhile was an unused substitute against Leeds earlier in the month but missed defeats against Como and Bournemouth due to a hip issue. No further details over his issue have been provided with his return date unknown.

Patrick Dorgu also remains on the sidelines as he recovers from a hamstring injury suffered in the September international break.

Rashford looks set to miss out again (Picture: Justin Setterfield/Getty Images)

Benjamin Sesko makes up United’s injury list with the Slovenia international expected to be ruled out until January at the earliest with a shin injury.

Sesko first suffered the shin problem in the spring, missing the entire pre-season campaign as part of his recovery. He started just two matches before the issue struck again in September.

‘We’re still not clear on exactly how long but it’s going to be a longer period of time,’ Carrick said this week.

Shaw has missed the last four games (Picture: Action Plus/Shutterstock)

‘It was the process and, as I said to you before, we had to go through the process, a bit of a healing process for the injury, to see how it would react, in what direction to go with that.

‘So, even now, we’re still working through it but, yeah, it’s unfortunate. Obviously, first and foremost, for Ben, for the kind of period that he’s had and tried to get through it.

‘Going through this again, and for us to not have him available, is obviously a blow, but we’ll try to get him back as quick as we can.’

United secured a huge victory in west London last season with Matheus Cunha’s goal helping them take a decisive step towards Champions League qualification. Premier League tickets on HelloTickets at Stamford Bridge are still available with six points separating the two clubs ahead of kick-off.

Official: Roma confirm 22-man squad for Udinese trip including five teenagers

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Gian Piero Gasperini has named a squad of 22 players including five teenagers for Roma’s upcoming trip to Udinese in Serie A. 

The Giallorossi are without several key players through injury, including Paulo Dybala, Manu Kone and goalkeeper. Lorenzo Pellegrini will also remain at home for the Udinese clash on Saturday. 

NAPLES, ITALY – OCTOBER 24: Gian Piero Gasperini AS Roma head coach before the Serie A match between SSC Napoli and AS Roma at Stadio Diego Armando Maradona on October 24, 2026 in Naples, Italy. (Photo by Francesco Pecoraro/Getty Images)

Pierluigi Gollini is expected to keep his place in goal in the absence of Svilar. He will be joined by understudies Devis Vasquez and 19-year-old Giorgio De Marzi. 

Four other teenagers have been included in the Giallorossi squad this weekend: Wing-back Emanuele Lulli (19), who has scored on his last two Serie A outings, summer signing Rodrigo Mora (19), plus academy products Alessandro Di Nunzio (19) and Mandjou Camara (17). 

ROME, ITALY – OCTOBER 27: Emanuele Lulli of AS Roma celebrates scoring his team’s first goal with teammate Gianluca Mancini during the Serie A match between AS Roma and Cagliari Calcio at Stadio Olimpico on October 27, 2026 in Rome, Italy. (Photo by Paolo Bruno/Getty Images)

Roma are one of three sides that remain unbeaten in Serie A after nine matches of the 2026-27 season. Their latest victory came against Cagliari earlier this week in an entertaining fixture that ended 4-2 in favour of the Giallorossi. 

They are set to take on an Udinese side that have lost six matches on the trot under Kosta Runjaic, which leaves them dangling above the relegation zone on five points.

Goalkeepers: Devis Vasquez, Giorgio De Marzi, Pierluigi Gollini
