West Ham had Simon Adingra on their radar to replace Crysencio Summerville but instead signed Manor Solomon.

West Ham had several players on their radar in the summer with interest mounting in Crysencio Summerville. The winger had interest from across the globe before eventually joining Al-Hilal for £60m.

One of the candidates to replace Summerville was Sunderland’s Simon Adingra. However, a deal never materialised and then WHUFC opted to make their move for Manor Solomon and he joined the club in August.

Photo: Arne Müseler / arne-mueseler.com / CC-BY-SA-3.0

Solomon has previous in the Championship and joined for a much lower fee than Adingra would have cost at the time. The Ivorian winger went on to join Ajax in the summer window.

How has Simon Adingra fared since West Ham moved on?

Adingra joined Ajax on a season-long loan on deadline day, with a non-mandatory option to buy reportedly worth around €16m. The 24-year-old was viewed as an exciting addition to Míchel’s attacking options, offering explosive pace and excellent one-on-one ability.

However, an ankle injury delayed his debut until October, when he managed around 30 minutes in a 1-1 draw against NEC Nijmegen. He registered one shot on target but has since suffered another setback, potentially keeping him out until after the November international break.

That leaves Adingra with very little opportunity to impress before January. His situation is particularly frustrating after an encouraging Monaco loan last season, when he recorded three goals and two assists in 14 Ligue 1 appearances.

His Sunderland spell was less successful, producing just one Premier League goal in 14 appearances. There is undoubtedly talent there, but recurring fitness problems and questions over consistency are becoming increasingly concerning. Ajax’s reported €16m purchase option now looks less straightforward.

The verdict: Manor Solomon has been a much better buy

Meanwhile, Solomon has made an encouraging start at West Ham. The 27-year-old arrived with proven Championship pedigree after recording 10 goals and 12 assists during Leeds United’s promotion-winning 2024/25 campaign.

He has contributed two goals and two assists in 12 league appearances this season, while creating 23 chances and completing 22 successful dribbles, according to FotMob. His performances have not always been spectacular, but his underlying numbers highlight his creative influence and ability to carry the ball into dangerous areas.

He has also demonstrated versatility across the forward line, making him a useful option for Nuno Espirito Santo. Adingra arguably offers greater natural explosiveness, but his injury record makes him a significant gamble.

Solomon, meanwhile, arrived at WHUFC for considerably less money and with proven second-tier experience. There is still time for Adingra to turn things around at Ajax, but West Ham’s decision to prioritise availability, experience and value for money is currently looking like the right one.
