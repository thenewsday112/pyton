Chelsea targeting Trent Alexander-Arnold as surprise transfer opportunity emerges

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Chelsea could be preparing for a significant change at right-back as the club assess their options ahead of the upcoming transfer windows.

With uncertainty surrounding Malo Gusto’s future at Stamford Bridge, the Blues have reportedly identified an unexpected target who could bring considerable experience and creativity to their defence.

Real Madrid defender Trent Alexander-Arnold has emerged as a potential option, with former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown suggesting the England international could be tempted by a move to west London if Chelsea decide to make an approach.

Chelsea Monitoring Trent Alexander-Arnold

According to Football Insider, Chelsea are keeping tabs on Alexander-Arnold as questions continue over his role at Real Madrid. The 28-year-old has struggled to establish himself as a regular starter under Jose Mourinho, with competition for places limiting his opportunities.

Brown revealed that Chelsea are among the clubs monitoring the situation, telling Football Insider: “One name who I have heard in connection with a move to Chelsea is Trent Alexander-Arnold.”

He admitted the potential transfer had initially surprised him, adding: “It came as a bit of a surprise to me, to be honest, but he has fallen well down the pecking order at Real Madrid to the point where he’s now the second or even third choice.”

The former scout also questioned whether the defender currently fits into Mourinho’s plans, explaining:

“It appears Mourinho doesn’t rate him particularly in that right-back position, and he’s not the only one, because he’s been out in the cold for England as well.”

Despite those concerns, Brown believes Alexander-Arnold remains an attractive player because of his technical ability and reputation. Chelsea, meanwhile, have reportedly been assessing potential replacements for Gusto as they consider changes to their defensive options.

Brown added: “Alexander-Arnold is a talented footballer who has a very good skillset, but it is those weaknesses in his game that often raises concerns.”

He also suggested that Chelsea’s interest in high-profile players could make the move possible: “Chelsea, though, will always be keen on the possibility of signing a big-name player; it’s just what they do, so they’ll be keeping an eye on whether he does become available.”

The possibility of a return to Liverpool has also been discussed, but Brown believes Stamford Bridge could become a realistic destination if the defender decides to leave Spain.

“They’ll want to avoid that situation coming up again, as will he I expect, so Chelsea could be where he ends up and he could be tempted if they decide to make a move.”

However, Alexander-Arnold is not currently pushing for an exit. Previous reporting has suggested he wants to fight for his place at Real Madrid, meaning any potential Chelsea move would depend on how his situation develops.

Alan Shearer stating: “I couldn’t give Eddie Howe any advice. I know the size of the football club and the football team are very different to what it was 20 or 30 years ago. ‘No confidence’ – Micah Richards tells Mikel Arteta to sell Arsenal star Viktor Gyokeres

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →
Former Manchester City defender Micah Richards (Picture: The Rest Is Football)

Micah Richards thinks Arsenal will have to look to sell Viktor Gyokeres if he is unable to rediscover his goalscoring touch for Mikel Arteta’s side.

Gyokeres arrived at the Emirates last summer, and amassed a respectable 22 goals in all competitions over the course of his debut season in English football.

However, the Swedish striker has clearly fallen down the pecking order at the start of the season, starting only one of Arsenal’s first eight games in the Premier League.

Gyokeres scored his first goal of the season in midweek against Fleetwood Town, but Richards believes the 28-year-old still looks short of confidence and faces an uncertain future at the Emirates.

‘I think one of the major talking points as well is Gyokeres,’ Richards said on The Rest Is Football podcast.

‘When he first arrived at Arsenal, I was saying to the Arsenal fans, “Don’t expect him to be scoring 25 and 30 goals”.

‘What I really liked about him, he was good at running in behind, but he stopped doing the things that he’s really good at.

Viktor Gyokeres has scored one goal so far this season (Picture: Getty)

‘He sort of looks like he has no confidence. I don’t know if the reports in the summer that they tried to get rid of him [were true] or not, but essentially he’s had enough game time to show what he can do now at Arsenal and it’s just not happening for him.’

Analysing how Arsenal might address Gyokeres’ lack of form when the transfer window reopens, Richards added: ‘I think in January, because of the injuries, I definitely think they should go back into the market, and find top cover around.

‘Because the way Arsenal play, and everyone talks about they need this player, they need that player, but the reason why they’re so good is what the front players do off the ball… so, Saka and Madueke work hard in a system that works for Arsenal.

Gyokeres has started only one Premier League match this season (Picture: Getty)

‘Obviously, losing Trossard was a big miss for them in terms of what he does off the ball and how clinical he was with the opportunities given to him.

‘But I think if they want to compete and then, say, dominate, then 100 per cent, I think they need more firepower in that forward area, for sure.

‘There are so many times where Gyokeres gets in really good positions, and I don’t know if he doesn’t back himself, I don’t know if it’s confidence, I don’t know if the team doesn’t back him to score, but he just, he doesn’t put the ball away. So, then it becomes a problem.

‘And I just feel like he needs one of those opportunities where he plays 90 minutes, he scores, he builds confidence, and it goes from there.

‘Otherwise, you know, they’re going to have to look to move him on.’

‘I think he can come off the bench and do a bit. I think he’s probably more of a squad player.’

Canny signing shows Nuno clearly knew something that Hammers fans did not | West Ham News

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Few West Ham fans took a great deal of interest over the deadline day loan signings made back in September after the relatively ‘big money’ deals – for the Championship, anyway – of Engels and Austbø had taken the headlines. West Ham needed a left back having sold El Hadji Malick Diouf and therefore when Adama Boiro arrived we naturally assumed he and Ollie Scarles would be battling it out for the left back slot.

Nottingham Forest Loanee Morato reveals surprising versatility – which a canny Nuno has exploited to the full at West Ham United

When in truth neither have had a look in. Few had done their homework on Morato who, first and foremost, had been a centre back at Nottingham Forest, but as he revealed today, had an extra string to his bow that a canny Nuno Espírito Santo has exploited to the full since the player’s arrival in September.

Morato’s ‘hidden talent’ revealed on West Ham’s web site today

According to whufc.com, the player revealed a level of expertise of which few were aware:

“I’m a centre-back by trade, but I played almost a whole season as a full-back with Benfica.

You really appreciate that versatility; it gives you extra motivation, knowing you can help the team in different positions. There aren’t a lot of centre-backs here so you might end up playing as a full-back rather than a centre-back.

I’m not the type of guy to be fussy about playing in one position, whether on the right or the left. For me, I think that helping the team by playing wherever the manager needs me. As a full-back, a centre-back or even a defensive midfielder. I’ll be ready to do it and try to do it as well as possible.”

As he’s shown, he might not be the typical build for a full back – there aren’t many left backs in this division who are 6 ft 4 and 14 stone (90kgs) and he might not be the fastest but he’s done a pretty decent job keeping the two regular left backs sat on the bench.

I’d quite like to see how he performs as a centre back – he’s one option for Nuno to use with Mavropanos injured – but the Brazilian is evidently happy to works his socks off for the team ‘wherever’ and repay the trust which his former Forest manager has once again show in him:

“[Nuno] gave me a lot of confidence, put a lot of trust in me and started me in loads of matches. He was always encouraging me. Those are the things that stick with you. Now we’re able to work together again and I hope it goes well.

It was a very brief conversation. He said he was counting on me, and I said I wanted to work with him again. It’s been good so far, and I’m going to do my very best to ensure that, without a doubt, we make a success of it.”

Severe rainfall and flood warnings issued for central Greece and western Turkey

📅 Oct 30, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Turkey has been hit by powerful thunderstorms and a fatal tornado this week with more heavy rain expected

Heavy rain and strong winds will continue to affect parts of south-eastern Europe and Turkey over the next few days, as an area of low pressure in the region has allowed for the formation of powerful thunderstorms. During a particularly intense thunderstorm in Antalya, a coastal city in the south-west of Turkey, the formation of a tornado resulted in the deaths of four construction workers on Tuesday. Authorities in Greece and Turkey have released severe rainfall and flooding warnings, as a further 100-150mm (nearly 4-6in) of rain is expected this week in central Greece and western parts of Turkey, with locally higher totals possible.

Travelling eastwards towards south Asia, strong winds and heavy rainfall have swept across the Indian regions of Odisha and Andhra Pradesh as Cyclone Arnab made landfall. With wind gusts in excess of 85mph (140km/h) and rainfall totals in some regions exceeding 200mm, the severe storm has prompted mass evacuations of more than 180,000 people, as well as widespread disruption to energy and transport infrastructure. The cyclone will weaken as it continues to traverse northwards over the coming days, though will continue to deliver heavy rain to parts of northern and central India as it does so.
