Arsenal have lost their last 16 games at Anfield, including seven in Arteta’s reign.</p> <p class="wp-block-paragraph">Whilst that may be the case, the current Liverpool team are very different to the one Arsenal have faced on a regular basis during Klopp’s time in charge.</p> <p class="wp-block-paragraph">They certainly can’t expect to be outplayed, but they must take advantage of Liverpool’s failings.</p> <p class="wp-block-paragraph">Arsenal are currently second in the Premier League with 21 points from eight games. They will want to win here but will need to do more than just run them into the ground.</p> <p class="wp-block-paragraph">If Rice and Lewis-Skelly can push forward and pressurise them, forcing them to make mistakes, then Arteta could finally have his winning recipe at Anfield.</p>
