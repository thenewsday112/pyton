Blackburn Rovers midfielder’s return from injury could be crucial after excellent first start of the season

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Blackburn Rovers midfielder Moussa Baradji earned his first start of the season against Wrexham last weekend following his return from injury.

Blackburn Rovers have made a steady start to the Championship campaign under Tony Mowbray, with five wins, three draws and four defeats from their opening 12 league fixtures. They sit 11th in the table with 18 points, just three points behind the play-off places, having scored 19 goals and conceded 16.

Their recent 2-0 victory over Wrexham provided another positive result, with Andri Gudjohnsen continuing to make an impression in attack. However, injuries have disrupted Mowbray’s plans at various points, making the return of several midfield options particularly important.

Photo by Chris Heaton, via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 2.0.

One player who could have a significant part to play is Moussa Baradji. The 25-year-old midfielder arrived from Swiss side Yverdon-Sport on loan in August 2025 before Blackburn made his move permanent this summer.

Baradji’s first season in English football offered plenty of encouragement but also raised questions about his consistency. He made 32 Championship appearances and his physical presence, ability to carry the ball and strength in aerial duels made him an interesting option.

A chance for a fresh start for Moussa Baradji under Tony Mowbray

A foot injury initially delayed his Blackburn debut until November 2025, while another setback during pre-season this summer proved particularly frustrating.

Having missed the previous preparation period through surgery, Baradji was determined to make an impression under Mowbray before a hamstring problem at Blackpool ruled him out for almost two months. His return came as a substitute against Portsmouth in September, but it was his first league start against Wrexham that provided the biggest encouragement.

Operating in a deeper midfield role alongside Sam Morsy, he delivered an impressive performance, playing almost the entire match and helping create the move for Gudjohnsen’s opening goal. The Lancashire Telegraph described it as his best performance for some time.

Mowbray has never hidden his admiration for Baradji’s potential. The Blackburn boss has praised his strength, athleticism, aerial ability and technical quality, even suggesting he possesses the attributes to become a ‘Premier League powerhouse’.

Moussa Baradji can become crucial to Blackburn Rovers

Mowbray has also challenged the midfielder to become more consistent and make himself undroppable through his performances in training and matches. That remains the biggest question surrounding Baradji.

His versatility allows him to operate as a number six, eight or 10, but finding his most effective position will be crucial. He has shown flashes of quality without consistently producing goals or assists, managing neither so far this season.

Nevertheless, his performance against Wrexham suggested there is considerably more to come. With Blackburn competing towards the top end of the Championship, Baradji’s combination of power, mobility and technical ability could provide Mowbray with another valuable midfield option.

If he can stay fit, establish a regular role and turn his obvious potential into consistent performances, the Frenchman could become an increasingly important figure in Blackburn’s promotion ambitions.

Oklahoma's SB 1589 makes running or supporting a dual-currency sweepstakes casino a Class C2 felony from November 1, after lawmakers overrode the governor's veto. Its reach into payment, geolocation and affiliate businesses raises the legal stakes for the whole sweepstakes supply chain.

Oklahoma’s sweepstakes casino ban starts on November 1, and operators are not waiting for the date. In recent days, brands such as High 5, McLuck, Hello Millions, SpinBlitz, PlayFame, Zula, Sportzino and Fortune Wins have either left the state or blocked its players.

The law’s reach down the supply chain sets it apart. Liability extends beyond operators to suppliers, payment processors, geolocation providers and affiliates. Tokens and similar currencies used in dual-currency models count as “representatives of value.” Tribal gaming approved under the federal Indian Gaming Regulatory Act is exempt.

Lawmakers overrode Governor Stitt to pass the ban

Senator Todd Gollihare authored SB 1589. It had its first Senate reading on February 2, 2026. On March 25, 2026, Representative Kyle Hilbert replaced Representative Fetgatter as its principal House author. The measure changes the scope of an existing gambling offense in the state’s crimes and punishments code.

Stitt’s veto letter objected that the bill needlessly created a new felony. He also opposed extending criminal liability to businesses and service providers. In his veto message, he argued that Oklahoma’s gaming laws should be clear, targeted and fair. SB 1589, he said, did not meet that standard.

The legislature disagreed. In the Senate, Gollihare moved that the bill become law despite the veto. The motion passed on a roll-call vote. The House then passed the measure over the veto by two-thirds of its elected members. That threshold is set by Article VI, Section 11 of the state constitution.

The provisions the governor objected to are now the ones that matter most for the industry. Businesses that serve sweepstakes brands, from payment firms to geolocation vendors, now face felony exposure in the state. That raises the legal risk across the sector, not just for the brands themselves.

Players face a different problem. The rules on remaining balances and pending redemptions are unclear. Oklahoma has no regulated online casino. As a result, legal online play in the state now goes only to tribal gaming.

Wyoming and Pennsylvania are considering similar bans

Other states are moving in the same direction. Pennsylvania has folded a sweepstakes ban into a broader gaming bill, HB 2801. Each violation by a sweepstakes operator could draw a fine from regulators if that bill passes. Pending bills in Pennsylvania do not carry over into 2027.

Wyoming’s draft would ban online casino and sweepstakes games under the state’s professional gambling law. The Select Committee on Gaming sent draft bill 27LSO-0098 to the Legislature’s Management Council. The council will decide whether to sponsor it in the 2027 session. If enacted, the Wyoming ban would take effect on July 1, 2027.

West Ham man’s 2.06 xG underperformance sparks concern for Nuno despite the Hammers’ attacking success

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

West Ham have largely performed well this season, but the form of Taty Castellanos is a cause for concern amid an xG underperformance of 2.06.

West Ham’s attacking prowess has been a standout of their campaign so far. WHUFC have scored the most goals in the league, with their tally of 32 being five more than league leaders Middlesbrough.

As was anticipated, Jarrod Bowen is leading the goalscoring for the Hammers, having scored six goals and laid on a further five assists in the Championship. Konstantinos Mavropanos has scored five goals too, proving to be a surprise threat in front of goal, mainly from set-piece situations.

Photo by Christine Matthews, via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC BY-SA 2.0.

However, the Irons could have scored more goals if they had more of a presence up front.

Joel Piroe has been a prolific marksman in the Championship over previous years, but has slightly struggled this season with three goals scored. Pablo Felipe draws praise for his work rate and hold-up play, but he’s yet to prove he can be a reliable goalscorer with one netted in eight league outings. However, the player whose form which has been the most surprising is Taty Castellanos, who was expected to star in the Championship.

The numbers behind Taty Castellanos’ struggles with West Ham

Castellanos has only scored one league goal this season – surprising, considering he netted six goals in 18 Premier League games last term, and has previously succeeded in Serie A (16 goals in 75 games) and La Liga (13 goals in 35 games).

The underlying numbers are where the concern arises though. The West Ham man has tallied up 3.06 xG, so his actual goal tally equates to an xG underperformance of 2.06. His 28 shots total puts him in the top 8% of Championship strikers, so he’s getting opportunities and failing to take them.

Castellanos is contributing with his link-up play, with three assists, a pass success rate of 80% (top 14%), and 15 chances created in total. However, there have been complaints over a lack of physicality and impact, which is where someone like Pablo makes up for his lack of goals.

In particular, the striker missed a guilt-edged opportunity against Southampton, a game lost by a single goal. In the Premier League last year, WHUFC’s Argentine striker showed his clinical abilities as he delivered on his 5.8 xG with six goals, but he has regressed in this area this term (all stats via Fotmob).

The Verdict: Nuno must find a solution in West Ham’s striker struggles

In West Ham’s last three matches, each of Nuno’s three strikers have gained a start. It shows the boss is still searching for his true no.9 to lead the charge for Championship promotion..

Although none of the three have performed particularly well, Nuno has to back one of them for a more consistent starting role. This could be Piroe, who has proven he can excel in this division.

Piroe was prolific in his time with Swansea City and Leeds United, and he is delivering closer to his xG with three goals from 3.47 xG (Fotmob). He’s a good all-rounder, adept at dropping into deeper areas to link up with WHUFC’s onrushing attackers and midfielders.

However, there is a current level of inconsistency with West Ham’s strikers, and that could prove to be an issue in the long run if they return to Premier League football. None are truly proven at the level, so they could look to reinvest at striker in the summer if promotion is secured.

In the short-term though, Nuno needs to find a solution by working with what he’s got.
